# A composed recipe is an ingredient

A recipe used inside another recipe is not a new kind of thing. It is an ordinary **Ingredient** whose optional **Reading** names a **Recipe** instead of a **Food** — `500 g de pâte à pizza`, with the pointer sitting where "what Food" would otherwise sit. The role that Ingredient plays is called a **Component**.

The link names a **Lineage**, never a Version, so it always resolves to whatever Branch of the dough the reader holds. How much of the inner recipe is wanted is **computed** by dividing the Reading's quantity by the inner recipe's **Yield**, and stored nowhere.

The second kind of link between recipes — "see also", "my mum's version of this" — is a separate and much lighter thing: a **Related Recipe**, untyped, two-way, held against the Lineage, and never travelling.

## Why

- **[ADR 0002](./0002-the-written-ingredient-line-is-the-truth.md) already decided this, and applying it costs nothing.** The written line is the truth and the understanding over it may be absent, wrong or unresolvable. A Component inherits that whole for free: delete the dough recipe, receive a bundle without it, hold no copy at all — the row still reads `500 g de pâte à pizza` and the pizza is still a correct recipe. A separate component list cannot degrade, because the pointer *is* the information; lose it and there is a hole where the dough was.
- **Every feature already walks the ingredient list.** Scaling, the Vault note, the sidecar, the share bundle, the PDF, the Backup, the Crouton importer, the shopping list. A third list beside Ingredients and Steps — the Mealie and Tandoor shape — means each of those learns about it separately, and each is a place it can be forgotten. Notably Tandoor's own export drops its composition links, which is exactly this failure happening in a shipped product.
- **Pinning a Version would corrupt the outer recipe's identity.** [ADR 0004](./0004-one-lineage-many-branches.md) names a Version by a fingerprint of its own content. If the pizza's content included the dough's fingerprint, editing the dough would mint a Version of the pizza nobody wrote, filling its history with saves that changed nothing — and two friends whose pizzas read identically but whose doughs differ slightly would be reported as diverged. Pointing at the Lineage leaves the sentence *"500 g of dough → that recipe"* unchanged when the dough changes.
- **Reproducibility is not lost, because it lives somewhere else.** [ADR 0005](./0005-an-attempt-is-a-version-you-cooked-but-did-not-keep.md) pins an Attempt to the Version on screen. "Which dough did I use that night" is a question for the cooking diary, not for the recipe.
- **Composition says *what*, never *when*.** Kamosu does not know that the dough is made the day before, so it must not splice the dough's Steps into the pizza's method. Where the timing matters, the cook writes a Step saying so — the same refusal to infer structure that [ADR 0002](./0002-the-written-ingredient-line-is-the-truth.md) and the no-timer-fields ruling in [#6](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/6) already make.

## Considered options

- **A third list on the recipe — "recipes used inside this one" — with a pointer and a quantity** (Mealie, Tandoor). Rejected: it is a new kind of object with its own display, storage, publication, import and export rules, it cannot degrade when the target is missing, and it duplicates a slot the Reading already has.
- **Pinning each Component to a specific Version of the inner recipe.** Rejected for corrupting the outer recipe's fingerprint, as above, and for turning every improvement to a base recipe into a chore across every recipe using it.
- **Storing an explicit multiplier on the link** ("× 0.5") beside the Ingredient Line. Rejected because it can silently contradict the words above it, and because it is another number to scale, store, publish, import and forget. The Branch Point of [ADR 0004](./0004-one-lineage-many-branches.md) set the precedent: compute, do not declare.
- **Typed and directional Related Recipes** — *variation of*, *goes with*, *replaces*. Rejected: the type must be chosen at the moment of linking, when the honest answer for two ratatouilles is that neither is the variation, and an unused taxonomy is harder to remove later than a plain list is to enrich.
- **An Ingredient whose Reading names a Recipe** (chosen).

## Consequences

- **A Component travels with the recipe that uses it, as a passenger.** Sharing the pizza — by link, bundle or PDF — carries the dough, because a recipe that cannot tell you how to make its own dough is incomplete. It does **not** change the dough's own **Visibility**: the dough gets no page, no Share Link of its own, and cannot be found. It is read *through* the pizza. Turning sharing on says so plainly on screen — *"this recipe includes Pizza Dough, which will be readable through this link"* — rather than as a dialog to dismiss. This holds at any depth and needs no exception in [ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md), because nothing sets a Visibility except a person deciding to.
- **A Related Recipe does not travel.** The distinction is whether the recipe is complete without the link. A Component is part of the recipe; a Related Recipe is a note about your own shelf, like an [Attempt](./0005-an-attempt-is-a-version-you-cooked-but-did-not-keep.md). Shipping them would either send dangling pointers to recipes the receiver has never held, or quietly haul a whole collection along with one recipe.
- **Scaling degrades politely and in both directions.** `500 g` of a dough yielding `1 kg` is ×0.5; `12 cookies' worth` of a recipe yielding `24 cookies` is ×0.5; a Yield's own noun is usable as a unit. No quantity — 28% of real ingredient rows have none, per [the Crouton export](../research/crouton-real-export.md) — means the whole recipe. `2 poignées de pâte`, or an inner recipe with no Yield at all, means no factor: the recipe is shown as written and Kamosu says it could not work it out. Scaling the outer recipe rescales the Reading, and the factor follows for free.
- **A Component unfolds in place rather than being navigated to.** Closed by default, opening to show the inner recipe's Ingredients and Steps already scaled, without leaving the recipe being cooked — one mechanism that serves both the owner and a stranger on a Share Link, who has no page to be sent to. Its Steps are never merged into the outer method. The layout is [#13](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/13)'s.
- **Cycles are never refused; unfolding stops instead.** A loop can be assembled from two halves on two servers and arrive already formed, so a save-time check cannot hold the line and would only give false confidence. Unfolding — on screen, into a shopping list, into a PDF — keeps track of what it has already opened and stops at a repeat, saying so. There is no depth limit beyond that.
- **Deleting a recipe another one points at needs no ceremony.** No cascade, no warning, no "3 recipes use this". The row stops being tappable and continues to read correctly.
- **A shopping list unfolds Components to the bottom**, applying the factor at each level under the same repeat-guard, and then merges Foods — which is a Food question, not a link question.
- **Import creates no links and suggests only one kind.** No source format carries composition or association, so every link is made by hand. After an import Job, Kamosu offers the recipes sharing a **Source** URL as candidate Related Recipes to tick — three such pairs exist in the real 86-recipe library — never linking automatically, because [#6](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/6) settled that Source is attribution and never identity. Composition is never guessed: matching `500 g plain flour` against a flour recipe is the kind of cleverness that is wrong more often than it is right.
- **The Vault note and the share bundle need a way to write a Component down** that survives a reader who does not have the inner recipe. The Ingredient Line carries the human meaning already; the Lineage id belongs in the `.kamosu/` sidecar of [ADR 0003](./0003-the-database-is-the-truth-the-vault-is-a-published-copy.md), alongside the Readings it sits in. An Obsidian `[[wikilink]]` on the line is the natural human rendering where the inner note is present in the same Vault. Exactly how a bundle carries its passengers is [#17](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/17)'s.
