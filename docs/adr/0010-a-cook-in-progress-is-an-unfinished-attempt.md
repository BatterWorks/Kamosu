# A cook in progress is an unfinished Attempt

Cooking mode — the step-by-step screen used with wet hands, at arm's length, on a phone propped against something — is not a thing of its own. It is an **Attempt** being lived rather than read back. Starting to cook creates the Attempt; the screen is that Attempt while it is still **In Progress**, and it is held on the server rather than in the browser, so the same cooking follows its cook from phone to iPad.

An In Progress Attempt additionally holds where the cook has got to: which **Step**, which **Ingredients** are ticked, and the **Yield** being cooked to. It is visible to its cook alone until it stops being In Progress, at which point [ADR 0005](./0005-an-attempt-is-a-version-you-cooked-but-did-not-keep.md)'s ordinary rule resumes and it inherits the recipe's visibility.

_(Amended by [ADR 0053](./0053-an-imported-attempt-names-its-cook-and-was-never-in-progress.md): an Attempt brought in from another app's cooking history is written finished, dated as that app dated it, and was never In Progress.)_

## Why

- **The language said so first.** [ADR 0005](./0005-an-attempt-is-a-version-you-cooked-but-did-not-keep.md) put *cook*, *session*, *make* and *bake* on Attempt's Avoid list, because those words already meant "one cooking of this dish". A second concept would have had no name left that did not already belong to Attempt — reliable evidence there was no second concept, only a state Attempt was missing.
- **Deviation and photographs happen mid-cook, and Attempt is already where they live.** "Three eggs, not two" is realised at the bowl, not in a form afterwards. Because the cook is already inside the Attempt, that lands directly in its **As Cooked** and a photograph lands directly on it. A separate live thing handing over at the end would have introduced a transfer, and every transfer is somewhere data is lost.
- **It is the stored-shape half of a question whose other half is free.** Under [ADR 0009](./0009-the-v1-cut-line-is-drawn-at-shape-not-at-features.md), what changes what is stored must be settled in v1. That a cook lives on the server is exactly that, and cannot be retrofitted. Everything computed on top of it — most obviously *who is cooking right now* in a Kitchen — costs the same later and is deliberately not in v1.
- **Parity delivers hands-free cooking without deciding anything.** Starting, advancing and finishing are Operations, so [ADR 0001](./0001-both-doors-generated-from-one-operation-catalogue.md) puts them at the MCP door automatically, and an agent asked to read out the next step is reading server state rather than a screen it cannot see. This is the concrete form of #12's finding that agents are the primary interface, and it arrived as a consequence rather than a feature.

## Considered options

- **Nothing is stored**: cooking mode is screen state and nothing else. Rejected because a phone that locks or a tab the browser evicts returns to step 1, with wet hands and a pan already going.
- **The device remembers**: progress in browser storage. Cheapest, and rejected because it cannot cross devices — starting on the phone and finishing on the iPad is the case that justified the cost at all.
- **A separate live entity that produces an Attempt at the end.** Rejected for the naming and hand-off reasons above.
- **A cook in progress is an unfinished Attempt, held on the server** (chosen).

## Consequences

- **An Attempt is real from the moment cooking starts, and never finishing it still counts as a cooking.** People do not file paperwork; they cook, they eat, and they walk away. Requiring a deliberate finish before a cooking counted would have made cook count and last-cooked date — promised by ADR 0005 — systematically wrong in a system whose purpose is remembering that you cooked this. Finishing remains deliberate, but it is where a rating or a note is attached, not where the cooking becomes true.
- **Deleting is the counterweight, and needs no new machinery.** Because starting counts, opening the steps out of curiosity would otherwise register as a cooking. ADR 0005 already made Attempts freely deletable by their cook, so undoing a false start is that same act, surfaced plainly in cooking mode. No new Operation and no new permission: ADR 0005's rule that anyone who may see a recipe may record an Attempt already covers starting to cook.
- **Kamosu stops offering to resume after three days without interaction, measured from the cook's last action rather than from the start.** A sourdough touched each day never ages out; a cook genuinely walked away from does. The interval decides only whether to offer to resume — never whether the cooking happened — so being generous costs a dismissed prompt, while being tight loses a place mid-bake.
- **The Yield being cooked to is held on the Attempt, and is not a deviation.** Doubling is a fact about one afternoon. Recording it as an **As Cooked** would mean Kamosu writing Ingredient Lines nobody wrote, against [ADR 0002](./0002-the-written-ingredient-line-is-the-truth.md), and would bury real deviations under routine doublings. This is not in tension with [ADR 0008](./0008-a-composed-recipe-is-an-ingredient.md)'s rejection of a stored multiplier: that multiplier sat on a *recipe*, inside a Component, where it could contradict the written line above it indefinitely. This one sits on an Attempt, never travels, and contradicts nothing, because the recipe still reads exactly as written.
- **One Attempt per person per Lineage may be In Progress at a time.** Two devices are one Attempt staying in step, the last device moved on being where the cook is. Allowing several would have meant asking *which cook do you mean?* on the one screen that must work at arm's length with wet hands — reintroducing the ambiguity server-side state was bought to remove. Two people cooking the same recipe at once was never in question: Attempts are per-person.
- **What this does not decide is what any of it looks like.** The wording of cancel, whether it warns when a photograph has already been taken, type size, tap targets and how a cook moves between steps are left to the prototype this unblocks.
