# A recipe is seen by its Kitchen, or by anyone with the link

A recipe is seen by the **Kitchen** that holds it. That is the whole of it: every member of that Kitchen reads it, cooks it and records an **Attempt** against it, and nobody else on the instance sees it at all.

_(Amended by [ADR 0041](./0041-a-recipe-is-written-in-a-cookbook-and-seen-in-a-kitchen.md): a recipe is seen by every Kitchen its Cookbook's Co-authors cook in, or by anyone with its link. The rejection of *Everyone here* and *Chosen* as settings on a recipe stands.)_

To put it in anyone else's hands you turn on its **Share Link**. They open a page and read it. To keep it, they take it into a Kitchen of their own — which is a **Copy**, exactly as it has always been.

There is no *Everyone here* and no *Chosen*. [ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md)'s four-level **Visibility** scale is reduced to a single fact — a Share Link exists, or it does not — and the term **Visibility** is retired with it.

## Why

- **A four-level scale kept a same-instance special case alive.** [ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md)'s central claim was that same-instance sharing, cross-instance sharing and import collapse into one rule, so [#16](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/16)'s never-merge needed no exception for two people on one box. It then wrote a scale in which *Chosen* and *Everyone here* did something no other server's user could do: put a recipe on someone's shelf without a file changing hands. The collapse was declared and then quietly not performed. Removing the two middle levels performs it: the person down the hall and the friend in another country take **the identical path** — link, read, take it into your Kitchen.
- **Nothing built on them.** The two levels appear in exactly three places across twenty-five ADRs and the glossary: one clause of the **Visibility** entry, one line of ADR 0007, and one passing mention in [ADR 0008](./0008-a-composed-recipe-is-an-ingredient.md) ("no listing under *Everyone here*"). No stored shape, no bundle field, no importer behaviour, no screen depends on either. This is the cheapest structural simplification available on the map.
- ***Everyone here* is a push nobody consented to.** An account on a home server is not a declaration of family. Give your brother an account so he can keep his own recipes and *Everyone here* lets any other account put recipes onto his Recipes screen unbidden — and lets him do it to you. The setting reads as "the household shelf" and is written as "everyone with an account", and those are the same thing only in the instance the author happens to be imagining. A rule that is right only for one household shape is not a rule.
- ***Chosen* buys nothing a link does not.** Naming a Person or a Kitchen to show them one recipe is a permission grant with a lifetime, a revocation story, and a question about what happens to their **Attempts** when it ends. A Share Link is a URL you send, and [ADR 0018](./0018-a-share-begins-at-the-beginning-and-there-is-no-unsay.md) already settled every hard question about it — it is public, permanent, expected to be reshared, and ending it reaches no copy already sent. Two mechanisms for one act, where the simpler one is already fully specified.
- **It makes the Recipes screen answerable.** With four levels, the library on screen was an unresolvable mix: your Kitchens' recipes, recipes aimed at you by name, and an instance-wide shelf whose size no one could predict. With one, the library is what your Kitchens hold, and every remaining question about it ([ADR 0027](./0027-the-library-is-one-shelf-and-a-result-says-what-matched.md)) has a clean answer.
- **Nothing is *aimed* at anyone.** The language the four-level scale invited — a recipe "shared with you", "sent to you", "aimed at you" — describes an act the model does not contain. A Kitchen holds a recipe; a link is a thing you hand over. Removing the middle levels removes the vocabulary that kept implying an inbox.

## Considered options

- **Keep the four levels** (*its Kitchen* / *Chosen* / *Everyone here* / *Anyone with the link*), as ADR 0007 wrote them. Rejected: two of the four are a same-instance shortcut that contradicts the collapse ADR 0007 claimed to have made, and *Everyone here* is a push onto strangers' screens.
- **Drop *Everyone here*, keep *Chosen*.** The tempting middle: the objectionable level is the one that reaches people you did not name. Rejected because *Chosen* then survives as a permission-grant mechanism serving one act that a Share Link already serves completely — carrying its own lifetime, revocation and Attempt questions for no capability.
- **Keep *Everyone here* as a shelf you visit rather than one you own** — an instance-wide library, findable but not mixed into your own. Rejected: it makes *Everyone here* barely distinguishable from a Share Link with extra steps, and it does not remove the push, it relocates it.
- **A recipe is seen by its Kitchen, or by anyone with its link** (chosen).

## Consequences

- ***Visibility* is retired from the glossary.** A term with two states, one of which is "a Share Link exists", is a word doing no work. **Share Link** carries it, and its entry says the rest.
- **Seeing means Kitchen membership.** [ADR 0005](./0005-an-attempt-is-a-version-you-cooked-but-did-not-keep.md)'s "anyone who can see a recipe may record an Attempt" now resolves to the Kitchen's members. Nobody reading a Share Link page records anything: the page is server-rendered HTML for a reader with no account ([ADR 0012](./0012-the-compiler-is-the-reviewer-the-frontend-does-not-have.md)), and to cook a recipe with a record kept, you take it into a Kitchen first.
- **The way to share with the person next to you is to share.** Turn on the link, send it. This is deliberately not free: it is one act, taken on purpose, rather than a setting that quietly widens an audience. The cost is that showing a housemate one recipe is now as much work as showing a friend abroad — accepted, because the alternative was a mechanism that only made sense when everyone on the box was family.
- **A Kitchen is the unit of sharing, and joining one is how people stop sharing files.** Two people who cook together should be in a Kitchen together; that is what a Kitchen is for. The removed levels were, in effect, a way to half-join a Kitchen, and [ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md) already rejected per-person grants for being unable to express co-authoring at all.
- **[ADR 0008](./0008-a-composed-recipe-is-an-ingredient.md)'s passenger rule is simplified, not changed.** A **Component** carried inside a shared recipe still gets no page and no Share Link of its own, and is readable only *through* its parent. The clause naming *Everyone here* drops away; the rule it stated does not.
- **[ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md) is amended, and its core survives intact.** The separation of *seeing* from *changing* — the thing that ADR was actually for — is untouched and in fact sharpened: inside your Kitchen you edit, outside it you **Copy**, with no third case in between. What is removed is the scale, not the distinction.
- **Nothing stored changes**, so under [ADR 0009](./0009-the-v1-cut-line-is-drawn-at-shape-not-at-features.md) this is reversible: a future version that wants an instance-wide shelf can add one over a library already written, without touching a recipe. Removing it now is the conservative direction — a setting never shipped is easier to add than one shipped and depended upon.
