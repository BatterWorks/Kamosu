# A Tag is how a Kitchen files, not what a recipe is

A Version's fingerprint does **not** cover **Tags**. Tagging a recipe, untagging it, renaming a Tag or merging two mints no Version and does not appear in the **Thread**.

_(Reopened by [ADR 0041](./0041-a-recipe-is-written-in-a-cookbook-and-seen-in-a-kitchen.md): recipes now belong to a **Cookbook**, and whether a Tag follows them there or stays a Kitchen's own filing is an open question on the build ticket. The argument below holds either way.)_

Tags continue to travel in a **Bundle** as names ([ADR 0020](./0020-a-bundle-is-one-recipes-worth-of-vault.md)), to land in the receiving **Kitchen**'s own list ([ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md)), and to be left out of a **Divergence** ([ADR 0019](./0019-an-ingredient-line-has-no-name-of-its-own.md)). What travels is unchanged; what is *named by the fingerprint* is what this settles.

This **sharpens** [ADR 0004](./0004-one-lineage-many-branches.md) rather than reversing it, exactly as [ADR 0021](./0021-a-reading-is-kamosus-reading-not-the-recipe.md) did for the **Reading**. `GLOSSARY.md` says a Version is named by a fingerprint of "the words and the Photographs" and never says whether a Tag is one of the words. It is not. [#51](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/51) was written the other way and is corrected here.

## Why

- **A Tag renames Kitchen-wide, and nothing that moves under a frozen Version may sit inside its name.** [#51](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/51)'s own requirement is that renaming a Tag "reaches every recipe carrying it, immediately" — that is the point of keeping Tags once per Kitchen rather than per recipe. Inside the fingerprint, tidying *dessert* to *desserts* silently renames every Version carrying it, in a Lineage `GLOSSARY.md` says is "never rewritten, never deleted". A person tidying their filing would be editing their own history without being asked and without being told.
- **[ADR 0019](./0019-an-ingredient-line-has-no-name-of-its-own.md) already decided this argument in a neighbouring case, and [ADR 0021](./0021-a-reading-is-kamosus-reading-not-the-recipe.md) decided it again.** Both refused something inside the fingerprint because it "manufactures a divergence out of nothing, in a system whose whole point is that identical states converge without communicating". Filing is precisely of that kind: Marc filing a recipe under *weeknight* and you not filing it is a difference between two filing systems, which [ADR 0007](./0007-a-recipe-is-held-by-a-kitchen-not-a-person.md) makes a feature rather than an accident — "what one Kitchen means by *quick* is its own business".
- **The manufactured divergence would also be invisible, which is worse than merely wrong.** [ADR 0019](./0019-an-ingredient-line-has-no-name-of-its-own.md) settled that Tags are not marked. So two identical recipes would report themselves diverged and the screen showing the divergence would have nothing on it to explain why — the same trap the Reading argument names, where "what is on screen is the same on both sides".
- **A Tag offers nothing stable to hash.** Its id belongs to one Kitchen and does not travel. Its names are per Language rather than split by Language ([ADR 0006](./0006-a-translation-is-a-branch.md)), so a fingerprint would have to pick one — and picking the writer's Language makes a Version's identity depend on who happened to save it, which is the [ADR 0015](./0015-a-name-is-not-identification-it-is-a-reminder.md) mistake in a new place. There is no third option: a Tag is a Kitchen's word for its own shelf, and a shelf is not portable.
- **The company outside the fingerprint is respectable and settled.** The Hand, the Version's name, its *what changed* line and its date sit outside it ([ADR 0015](./0015-a-name-is-not-identification-it-is-a-reminder.md)); the Reading sits outside it ([ADR 0021](./0021-a-reading-is-kamosus-reading-not-the-recipe.md)). Tags join a list that already exists, so no new kind of exception is created here.
- **What the alternative bought was small.** Inside the fingerprint, retagging would appear in the Thread as a change. That is a nice line in a history screen, weighed against the one mechanism the whole map stands on — and it is a line about the shelf rather than about the dish, on a screen [ADR 0023](./0023-a-sheet-carries-the-recipe-not-the-library.md) has already ruled Tags off the paper equivalent of.

## Considered options

- **Tags inside the fingerprint**, as [#51](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/51) was written. The intuitive answer: a Tag is a word on the recipe, so changing it changes the recipe. Rejected — a Kitchen-wide rename would re-fingerprint frozen Versions, and two Kitchens filing differently would diverge over nothing.
- **Tags inside the fingerprint, frozen per Version** — each Version hashing the Tag names as they read at the moment of saving. Fixes the re-fingerprinting, and rejected anyway: it means an old Version keeps the old spelling forever, which is the split-per-recipe Tag list [#51](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/51) exists to abolish, arriving through the back door.
- **Tags inside the fingerprint by id rather than by name.** Rejected: a Tag id belongs to one Kitchen and is meaningless in the next one, so two holders of the identical recipe would never agree — the failure [ADR 0021](./0021-a-reading-is-kamosus-reading-not-the-recipe.md) found in a portable Food id, in a sharper form.
- **Tags outside the fingerprint** (chosen).

## Consequences

- **Retagging mints no Version and is invisible in the Thread, on purpose.** It is a change to how a Kitchen files the dish, not to the dish, and the person who made it sees the new Tag on the recipe.
- **Opening an old Version shows today's Tags.** The Thread lets a person open any Version and cook from one; the words and Photographs there are March's, the filing around them is now. The accepted loss, and the right one: filing is present-tense, and nobody reading back wants the shelf they have since emptied.
- **A recipe's stored content shape gains nothing.** The fields a fingerprint covers were fixed by [#43](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/43) and this ADR leaves them alone, so no Version already saved changes its id. Had the answer gone the other way, every one of them would have.
- **[#51](https://github.com/BatterWorks/Kamosu/issues/51) builds Tags as a Kitchen-side list pointed at by recipes**, joined to a recipe from outside its content rather than stored within it. Its fifth acceptance criterion is corrected to say so.
- **A Bundle is unaffected.** Tags travel as names and land in the receiving Kitchen's list; a Tag altered in transit changes nothing anyone can verify and nothing anyone relies on, because a Tag makes no claim about the dish.
- **This is [ADR 0019](./0019-an-ingredient-line-has-no-name-of-its-own.md)'s rule one layer down.** That ADR kept Tags out of what a divergence *shows*; this one keeps them out of what a Version *is*. Same reason both times — a Kitchen's filing is not the recipe.
