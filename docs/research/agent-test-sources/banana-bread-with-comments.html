Brown butter banana bread

The bananas on our counter go black faster than anyone eats them, so this loaf gets made most weeks. Browning the butter first takes five extra minutes and makes it taste of toffee.

Makes one 900 g (2 lb) loaf, about 10 slices.

Ingredients

  • 115 g unsalted butter
  • 3 very ripe bananas (about 350 g peeled)
  • 150 g light brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 220 g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 75 g walnuts, chopped

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C (160 °C fan) and line a 900 g loaf tin.
  2. Melt the butter in a small pan and keep it on a medium heat, swirling, until it smells nutty and the bits at the bottom turn brown, about 5 minutes. Pour into a large bowl and leave to cool for 10 minutes.
  3. Mash the bananas into the butter, then beat in the sugar, eggs and vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt until no flour shows, then fold in the walnuts.
  5. Scrape into the tin and bake for 40 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then on a rack.
