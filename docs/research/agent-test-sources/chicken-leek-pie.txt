Chicken, leek and tarragon pie

Serves 4 to 6

For the filling
2 tbsp olive oil
two leeks, sliced
600 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
1 tbsp plain flour
150 ml dry white wine
300 ml chicken stock
4 tbsp crème fraîche
a handful of tarragon, chopped
salt and pepper to taste

To finish
1 quantity rough puff pastry (see below)
1 egg, beaten

Method
1. Warm the oil in a wide pan and cook the leeks gently for 10 minutes until soft. Add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes more.
2. Stir in the flour, then the wine. Let it bubble for 2 minutes, then add the stock and simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Stir in the crème fraîche and tarragon, season, and leave to cool completely.
4. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Spoon the filling into a 1.5 litre pie dish. Roll the pastry out to 5 mm thick, lay it over the dish and trim. Brush with the egg and cut a small hole in the middle.
5. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until the pastry is deep golden.

Rough puff pastry

Makes enough for one pie

250 g plain flour
½ tsp salt
250 g cold butter, cut into 1 cm cubes
about 150 ml iced water

1. Mix the flour and salt in a bowl and toss in the butter. Add the water a little at a time and bring it together into a rough, lumpy dough.
2. On a floured surface, roll into a rectangle about 20 x 50 cm. Fold the top third down and the bottom third up, give it a quarter turn, and roll out again.
3. Repeat the folding and rolling three more times, then wrap and chill for at least 30 minutes before using.
