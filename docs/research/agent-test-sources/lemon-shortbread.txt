Lemon shortbread

Makes 16 fingers

225 g butter, softened
110 g caster sugar
finely grated zest of 1 lemon
325 g plain flour
1 tbsp demerara sugar

1. Preheat the oven to 18 °C (350 °F) and grease a 20 x 30 cm tin.
2. Beat the butter, caster sugar and lemon zest together until pale.
3. Work in the flour until the dough just comes together, then press it evenly into the tin.
4. Prick all over with a fork, sprinkle with the demerara sugar and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until pale golden.
5. Cut into 16 fingers while still warm, then leave to cool in the tin.
