Shorbet Ads (Egyptian red lentil soup)

A smooth, golden soup eaten all over Egypt in winter. Serve it with toasted pita and a squeeze of lemon.

You will need

  • 200 g (1 cup) red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon ground coriander
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 litre (4 cups) vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped parsley, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over a medium flame and cook the onion until soft and lightly golden.
  2. Add the garlic and cook one minute more.
  3. Stir in the lentils and all the spices so that every lentil is coated.
  4. Pour in the stock, bring to the boil, cover and simmer on low for 20 to 25 minutes, until the lentils fall apart.
  5. Blend until smooth, return to the heat, add the lemon juice and season.
  6. Serve hot, scattered with parsley.
