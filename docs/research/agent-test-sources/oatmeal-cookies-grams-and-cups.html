Oatmeal raisin cookies

Makes 18 cookies. Prep 15 minutes, bake 10 to 12 minutes.

IngredientMetricCups and spoons
Butter, softened115 g½ cup
Brown sugar150 g¾ cup, packed
Egg11
Vanilla extract5 ml1 tsp
Plain flour125 g1 cup
Bicarbonate of soda2.5 g½ tsp
Salt1.5 g¼ tsp
Rolled oats135 g1½ cups
Raisins80 g½ cup

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C (350 °F) and line two baking trays.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, then beat in the egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt, then the oats and raisins.
  4. Roll into 18 balls, set them 5 cm apart on the trays and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden at the edges.
  5. Leave on the trays for 5 minutes, then move to a rack to cool.
