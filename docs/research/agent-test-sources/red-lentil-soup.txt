Red lentil soup with cumin

Serves 4

2 tbsp olive oil
1 large onion, chopped
2 carrots, diced
2 cloves garlic, crushed
1 tsp ground cumin
200 g red lentils, rinsed
1.2 litres vegetable stock
1 lemon, juiced
Salt and pepper

Warm the oil in a large pan and soften the onion and carrots for 8 minutes.
Add the garlic and cumin and cook for 1 minute more.
Tip in the lentils and stock, bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils have collapsed.
Blend until smooth, stir in the lemon juice and season with salt and pepper.
