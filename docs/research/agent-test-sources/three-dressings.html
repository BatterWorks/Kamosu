Three dressings for the week

I make all three on Sunday and keep them in jars in the fridge door. Each keeps for five days.

Lemon tahini dressing

Makes about 200 ml.

  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 5 tbsp cold water
  • ½ tsp salt
  1. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice and garlic together. It will seize and thicken.
  2. Whisk in the water a spoonful at a time until it pours like double cream.
  3. Season with the salt and taste.

Honey mustard vinaigrette

Makes about 150 ml.

  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 6 tbsp olive oil
  • A pinch of salt
  1. Shake everything in a jar with the lid on for 30 seconds.
  2. Shake again before each use.

Buttermilk herb dressing

Makes about 250 ml.

  • 150 ml buttermilk
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • Black pepper
  1. Stir the buttermilk into the mayonnaise until smooth.
  2. Stir in the herbs and vinegar, and season with pepper.
  3. Chill for 1 hour before using.
