THE Shakshuka That Got Me Through Last Winter (Easy, One Pan!!)

Okay friends. Pull up a chair, because before we get to the eggs I need to tell you about the worst February of my life.

It started with the boiler. You know the sound a boiler makes right before it gives up completely? A sort of tired sigh? Ours sighed on a Tuesday, and by Thursday we were wearing hats indoors and I was standing over the stove at seven in the morning just to be near something warm. That is where this shakshuka was born. Well, not born. People have been making shakshuka for a very long time across North Africa and the Middle East and I am not claiming to have invented anything. But it was born for ME, that week, in that cold kitchen.

I had a tin of tomatoes, two sad peppers, an onion and half a box of eggs. That's it. And honestly? That is all you need. My partner wandered in, looked at the pan and said "is that breakfast or dinner" and the answer, my loves, is YES.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE THIS RECIPE
- One pan! Fewer dishes!!
- Pantry ingredients you probably already have
- Ready in about 30 minutes
- Breakfast, lunch, dinner, it doesn't care

A NOTE ON PEPPERS: I like red peppers best because they go sweet and jammy, but a yellow one is lovely too. Green ones are a bit bitter here, skip those if you can.

A NOTE ON SPICE: I use cumin, smoked paprika and a pinch of chilli flakes. If you have harissa, a spoonful stirred in is incredible. My mum swears by a little caraway but she also puts caraway on everything so take that with a grain of salt (ha).

[ADVERTISEMENT]

Can I make it ahead? Kind of! Make the sauce up to two days ahead and keep it in the fridge, then warm it up and add the eggs when you want to eat.

Can I freeze it? The sauce, yes. The eggs, please no.

What do I serve it with? Crusty bread. Lots of it. Toast also works. My partner likes it with rice, which is chaotic, but fine.

SUNNY SIDE SHAKSHUKA
Serves 2 (or 1 very cold person)
Prep 10 mins | Cook 20 mins

Ingredients
2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, sliced
2 red peppers, sliced
3 cloves garlic, sliced
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp smoked paprika
pinch of chilli flakes (more if you're brave)
1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
1 tsp sugar, only if your tomatoes are sharp
salt and pepper
4 eggs
a handful of parsley or coriander, chopped
feta, crumbled, to serve (optional but not really)

Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a wide frying pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and peppers with a pinch of salt and cook for about 10 minutes until soft and starting to colour.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli flakes and stir for a minute until it smells amazing.
3. Tip in the tomatoes (and sugar if using), season, and simmer for 8-10 minutes until thick enough that a spoon leaves a trail.
4. Make 4 little wells with the back of a spoon and crack an egg into each one.
5. Put a lid on and cook on low for 5-8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny.
6. Scatter over the herbs and feta and bring the pan straight to the table.
